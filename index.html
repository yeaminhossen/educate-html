How it works

From first click to certificate—
four simple steps

Every stage is spelled out so you always know what comes next. Learn on your schedule; pause or resume anytime.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Find the right course

    Explore topics, compare outlines, and read learner reviews before you commit.

    • Filter by skill level and course length.
    • Preview lessons where available—no guesswork.
  2. Step 2 of 4

    Watch & learn your way

    Clear video lessons and notes you can replay whenever you need a refresher.

    • Progress saves automatically across devices.
    • Phone, tablet, or desktop—same account everywhere.
  3. Step 3 of 4

    Practice with real tasks

    Short quizzes and projects turn theory into skills you can use on the job.

    • Downloadable files and briefs included.
    • Get feedback on assignments on supported plans.
  4. Step 4 of 4

    Earn & share your certificate

    Complete the track, pass the final check, and download proof you can share anywhere.

    • Add to LinkedIn or your portfolio in one click.
    • Keep access to materials while your plan is active.
